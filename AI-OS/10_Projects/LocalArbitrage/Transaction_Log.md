Record **failed** buys too — items that didn't sell, sold below estimate, or turned out broken. A log of only wins teaches nothing and quietly inflates your confidence.

## What to Look For (after ~20 entries)
- Which **category** produces the best €/hour — not best margin %, best €/hour
- Which **search signal** (`Haushaltsauflösung` vs `muss weg` vs misspellings) actually converts
- What **distance** stops being worth it
- Which items **sit** — days-to-sell is a cost, not a footnote; €50 tied up for 6 weeks isn't €50
- Where the [[Valuation_Method]] estimates were *wrong*, and in which direction

## Why This Matters More Than It Looks
The flips are trading time for money. The dataset is the thing that stops being that: once you know "DeWalt tools under €80 within 30km reliably return 40%+," you're not searching anymore, you're executing a known pattern. That's the only part of this project that gets easier over time.
