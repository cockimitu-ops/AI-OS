## This Is Gewerblich, Not Private
Buying with the intent to resell, repeatedly, is commercial activity (gewerblich) under German law — regardless of scale, and regardless of whether it feels like a hobby. Occasional sale of your own belongings is private; systematic buy-to-resell is not. That distinction is the whole thing.

Consequence: **Gewerbeanmeldung required at the start of the activity**, not once it's profitable. €22–112 in Crimmitschau. Plus the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung via ELSTER within one month.

## The Threshold That Actually Bites
**€565/month — Familienversicherung.** Cross it and free family health insurance can end, costing roughly €150/month in own contributions. That single number can eat an entire month's arbitrage profit and then some.

This project is more likely to hit it than anything else in the vault, because gross revenue moves fast even when profit doesn't. Worth knowing precisely how it's assessed (income vs. profit) **before** approaching it — ask the Krankenkasse in writing, don't guess.

## DAC7 Platform Reporting
Kleinanzeigen, eBay, and similar report seller data automatically to the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern past **30 sales OR €2,000 revenue per year**. At 2–4 flips a month you cross 30 sales within a year without noticing. Being reported isn't itself a problem if the activity is properly registered — it's only a problem if it isn't.

## Differenzbesteuerung — Not Available Here
§25a UStG (paying VAT only on the margin rather than the full sale price) exists specifically for used-goods resellers and sounds tailor-made for this. **It does not apply to Kleinunternehmer under §19** — you can't tax a margin when you aren't charging VAT at all. Only becomes relevant if revenue ever exceeds the €25,000 Kleinunternehmer threshold. Noted so it isn't planned around prematurely.

## Practical Sequence
1. Test the model with a handful of flips to see whether it's even worth it
2. If continuing → Gewerbeanmeldung + ELSTER registration promptly
3. Keep every receipt: purchase price, fuel, repair parts, listing fees — all deductible
4. Watch the €565 line before it arrives, not after

## Honest Note
Plenty of people flip casually without registering. That's a real risk being taken, not a loophole — and the consequences (back taxes, fines) land on the person, not the plan. Worth deciding deliberately rather than by default.
