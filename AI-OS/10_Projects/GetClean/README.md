## Why
Health, mental clarity ahead of September's studies, and cost — not one single reason, a real combination.

## Scope, On Purpose
Cannabis only, right now. Regular use. Nicotine and psilocybin are explicitly set aside — not ignored, just not the current fight. Revisit later, once this one's steady.

## What to Actually Expect
Cannabis withdrawal isn't medically dangerous, but it's real: irritability, disrupted sleep, lower appetite, restlessness, cravings. Usually starts within 1–2 days, peaks around days 2–6, mostly settles within two weeks — sleep can take longer to fully normalize. Knowing this ahead of time matters: it's easy to mistake normal withdrawal for "this isn't working" right when it's about to get easier.

## What This Project Won't Do
No tapering schedule, no dosing guidance — that's genuinely not something to get from a Markdown file. If it gets hard, a Suchtberatung (German addiction counseling, free, confidential, no parental involvement needed at 18) is real backup, not a last resort.

## Milestones

## Tracking
Simple, not obsessive: day count, and a one-line note on mood/sleep if useful. Not a log to feel judged by.
