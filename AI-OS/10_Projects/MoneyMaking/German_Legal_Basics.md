## Not Legal or Tax Advice
This is a summary of what came back from research, not verified against official sources directly by Claude, and not a substitute for asking a Steuerberater or the actual Gewerbeamt/Finanzamt before anything with real money or legal weight happens. Treat it as a starting map, not a final answer.

## Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG)
Since 2025: a prior-year threshold of €25,000 net revenue and a current-year threshold of €100,000 — the current-year one applies immediately on being exceeded, not from the following year. At small-scale income levels this applies cleanly: no VAT charged on invoices, no input tax deduction, and since 2024 no annual VAT return required either.

## Gewerbeanmeldung (Business Registration)
Any activity aimed at profit and set up as ongoing counts as a Gewerbe, regardless of whether it actually turns a profit. Registration is required immediately at the start of the activity — delays risk fines. Ad/affiliate content, UGC ads, print-on-demand, and digital products are treated as commercial (gewerblich), not freelance (freiberuflich), in essentially all cases. The Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung must be filed electronically via ELSTER within one month of starting (§138 Abs. 1b AO) — the ELSTER certificate arrives by post, so registering early matters.

## Einkommensteuer (Income Tax)
The 2026 basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag) is €12,348 — no income tax below that, but a filing obligation (Anlage EÜR) still exists once there's business income at all. Keep receipts for tools, equipment shares, and the registration fee as deductible business expenses.

## Kindergeld
During a first Ausbildung or first degree, there's no income limit — parents keep full Kindergeld regardless of what's earned. The 20-hour rule only applies starting with a second Ausbildung, and even then it's about hours, not income level.

## Krankenversicherung — Familienversicherung
**The number that matters most for anything that scales:** free family insurance coverage holds as long as self-employment stays secondary (under 20 hours/week) and monthly income stays under **€565/month**. Crossing that threshold risks roughly €150/month in independent contributions. This is a bigger practical brake on scaling than tax or business registration — confirm with the Krankenkasse in writing before approaching it, not after.

## DAC7 / Platform Tax Transparency
Etsy, eBay, Vinted, and similar platforms automatically report seller data to the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern once a seller passes **30 sales or €2,000 in revenue** in a year. Being reported doesn't automatically mean tax is owed for genuine private occasional sales — but a real commercial activity still needs proper registration regardless of whether DAC7 reporting triggers.

## Local Note
Registration costs specifically in Crimmitschau were quoted at €22–112 for a simple sole-proprietorship Gewerbe — confirm the exact fee directly with the local Gewerbeamt before budgeting against it, since that's a meaningful chunk of a €50 starting budget at the higher end.
