Two rounds of research (v1: side-income only, rejected as too small; v2: opened to a legitimate startup, well-received) produced a ranked list of five options in [[Candidate_Options]]. Options 1, 3, and 4 were approved for active pursuit and graduated into their own projects, since each has a genuinely different shape of work and timeline:

- **Option 1 (productized AI content agency)** → [[10_Projects/ContentAgency/README|ContentAgency]]
- **Option 3 (sell system templates)** → [[10_Projects/TemplateSales/README|TemplateSales]]
- **Option 4 (funding, regardless of business model)** → [[10_Projects/FundingApplications/README|FundingApplications]]
