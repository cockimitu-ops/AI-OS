## Description

**You picked a price because it felt about right. Now someone's asked why
it isn't 30% higher, and you don't have an answer.**

Pricing is the decision solo founders make fastest and revisit least. You
glance at two competitors, split the difference, and ship. Six months later
you're either leaving money on the table or wondering why nobody converts —
and you have no idea which, because you never wrote down what would tell you.

This is the six-module process that ends in a number you can defend, plus
the specific signals that would tell you to change it.

**What's inside:**
- A duplicate-ready Notion template, six modules
- Every AI prompt pre-written — free tiers of Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini all work
- A worked example: a real product priced end to end, with real competitor data
- A decision framework for the value metric question most founders never ask

**The six modules:**
1. **Price Mapping** — what the market charges, and what sits behind each paywall. The gating logic tells you more than the numbers.
2. **Value Metric Discovery** — what you charge *per*. The highest-leverage decision in pricing and the one most often made by accident.
3. **Willingness to Pay** — the cheap version of a pricing study, built on what your buyer already spends elsewhere.
4. **Tier Architecture** — how many tiers, what separates them, and what you're excluding that buyers will actually accept.
5. **Anchoring and Signal** — where your price sits, what that claims about you, and whether you can back the claim.
6. **Launch Price and Iteration Plan** — a specific number, and six falsifiable signals with a 60-day deadline.

**This is for you if:**
- You're about to launch and the price is still a guess
- You've been charging the same thing for a year without knowing if it's right
- You copied a competitor's tiers without asking what they're charging *per*

**This isn't for you if:**
- You want a pricing calculator — this is a research and reasoning process, not a formula
- You need enterprise or sales-led pricing strategy — this is built for self-serve products

**Format:** Notion template + PDF prompt pack + worked example.
**Delivery:** Instant download.

## FAQ

**Do I need competitor research done first?**
It helps. Module 1 assumes you have a competitor list. If you don't, the
Micro-SaaS Moat Blueprint produces exactly that — and its output is this
template's input.

**Is this only for SaaS?**
The value-metric and tier modules are strongest for subscription products.
Modules 1, 3, 5 and 6 work for anything you price, including services and
one-off digital products.

**Will it tell me my exact price?**
It'll get you to a specific number with reasoning attached, and tell you what
would prove it wrong. No process can promise the number is right — anyone
selling you that is selling confidence, not analysis.

**How long does it take?**
60–90 minutes for a first pass.
