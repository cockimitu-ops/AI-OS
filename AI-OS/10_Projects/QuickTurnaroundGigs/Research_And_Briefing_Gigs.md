## Description
```
Get a clear, investor-ready view of your startup's competition — fast.

I specialize in competitor analysis for early-stage startups. You'll get a concise, citation-backed report that shows:
- Who your real competitors are (direct, indirect, and emerging)
- How they position, price, and sell their product
- What customers love and complain about
- Where you can differentiate and win

What you'll receive:
- A structured competitor analysis report (PDF)
- Comparison tables (features, pricing, positioning)
- SWOT analysis for each competitor
- A short executive summary with 3–7 concrete actions for the next 30–90 days

Why this matters for startups: clarifies your positioning before you build or pitch, helps you answer "who are your competitors" and "why will you win" in investor meetings, saves you 10+ hours of manual research.

My process: I identify your most relevant competitors (or use your list), research their product, pricing, positioning, and recent moves using AI-powered research tools with citations, then synthesize everything into a clear, strategy-focused report for your stage and goals.

Perfect for pre-seed and seed startups validating their idea, founders preparing for investor meetings, and early teams deciding on pricing, features, or positioning.

Order now and get a clear picture of your competitive landscape in days, not weeks.
```
