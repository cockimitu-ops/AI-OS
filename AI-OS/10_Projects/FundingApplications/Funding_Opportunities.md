## InnoStartBonus (Saxony) — the standout find
A non-repayable grant: **€1,050/month for up to 12 months (up to €12,600 total)**. Eligible: natural persons 18+, primary residence in Saxony — explicitly *without* requiring university enrollment. This solves the actual bottleneck better than any single business idea does: not capital for tools, but runway to work on something for months without a side job.

- Application: competitive process through futureSAX (concept paper, video, presentation).
- **Open question, worth resolving first:** whether student (Schüler) status specifically qualifies. Contact directly: gruender@futuresax.de, 0351 79997979.
- This is the single most important immediate action, regardless of which business option gets pursued — see [[10_Projects/MoneyMaking/README|MoneyMaking]]'s Immediate Next Actions.

## JUGEND GRÜNDET — no real downside
Business plan competition for students/apprentices up to 23. Free. Can count as a *besondere Lernleistung* (special academic achievement) toward the Abitur.

## Also found, lower near-term relevance
- **Sächsischer Gründerpreis** — prize money up to €60,000, with a NextGen category.
- **Youth Start-Up Challenge** (EU-wide, ages 18–25).
- **EXIST-Gründungsstipendium** (up to €2,500/month plus material/coaching allowances) — not accessible now, requires university/research-institution affiliation, but becomes relevant once a degree program starts, even for the same venture.
- **KfW StartGeld** (up to €200,000) — unrealistic short-term without income/credit history via a Hausbank; a later-stage instrument.

## Legal Form
Einzelunternehmen/Kleingewerbe is sufficient to start (€0 minimum capital). A UG is technically possible from €1 share capital but realistically costs ~€400–530 to actually form, plus ongoing bookkeeping — worth it once real liability exists (B2B contracts, customer data, employees), not before. A GmbH (€25,000 capital) isn't relevant at this stage.
