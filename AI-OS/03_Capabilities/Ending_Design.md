## What It Does
Writes the closing resolution of the final part, applying [[Storytelling_Fundamentals]]'s change/transformation principle and [[Emotional_Engagement]]'s earned-investment rule. Distinct from [[Retention_Beat_Scripting]] (paces the body) and [[Cliffhanger_Creation]] (owns non-final endings) — this capability owns only the story's actual conclusion.

## Inputs
The final part's drafted body, and the story's originally identified change/conflict, from [[Story_Ideation]].

## Outputs
A resolution that completes the change identified at ideation.

## Production Note
See [[Cliffhanger_Creation]]'s production note on CTA timing — the same principle likely applies to a final part's own outro/CTA (don't let it overlay before the last line finishes), though this hasn't been tested on a final part specifically yet, only on a non-final cliffhanger.

## Success Criteria
The resolution answers the specific question the story's hook opened — an ending that resolves a different question than the one the hook asked fails, even if it's satisfying on its own.

## Validation
Does the ending trace back to the hook's open loop, and does it deliver payoff proportional to the stakes established during the story, per [[Emotional_Engagement]]?

## Analytics Reference
Full-completion rate — viewers who watch to the actual end — measures this capability directly via [[Metrics_Framework]]; recorded in [[Ending_Database]].
