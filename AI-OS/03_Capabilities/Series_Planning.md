## What It Does
Sequences which validated stories get produced in what order, and tracks recurring patterns worth deliberately varying — e.g., not publishing three suspense-heavy stories back to back. Operates above [[Story_Drafting]] and every per-story capability; it doesn't touch an individual story's content, only the order and mix of stories across a production calendar.

## Inputs
A pool of validated stories, from [[Story_Validation]], each with its identified premise type.

## Outputs
A production order/calendar, with rationale for the sequencing.

## Success Criteria
No single premise type or emotional register, per [[Emotional_Engagement]]'s variety principle, repeats more than twice consecutively in the published sequence, unless deliberately noted as an exception with a reason.

## Validation
Does the planned sequence show variety in premise type and emotional register, and is every deliberate exception to that actually justified in writing rather than just present?

## Analytics Reference
Whether sequencing variety actually affects channel-level retention, not just per-story retention, is a [[Continuous_Improvement_Cycle]] question — the first one in this system operating above individual [[Review_Process]] runs.

## Knowledge Dependencies
[[Emotional_Engagement]] — variety principle, applied at the series level.
