## Core Principles
- Pacing is the relationship between story time (how much happens) and telling time (how long it takes to tell it) — fast pacing compresses story time into less telling time.
- Uniform pacing reads as monotonous even when individual beats are strong; variation in intensity is what makes rhythm perceptible at all.
- Every beat should either escalate or resolve. A beat that does neither is a pacing dead zone.

## Key Concepts
- Story time vs. telling time
- Rhythm through variation
- Dead zones — beats that neither escalate nor resolve
