## Core Principles
- A story is a change: someone or something moves from one state to another, driven by conflict or tension. Without change, a sequence of events is a report, not a story.
- Specificity outperforms generality — concrete, particular detail engages more than abstract summary.
- An audience invests attention only once it has something to want: a question, an outcome, a resolution.

## Key Concepts
- Change / transformation
- Conflict — what makes the change non-trivial
- Specificity over abstraction
- Audience investment as a precondition for engagement
