## Core Principles
- Retention is decided at every point where the audience could stop, not just at the start. Each such point needs its own reason to continue — not just momentum carried over from the hook.
- An unresolved open loop is the single strongest retention mechanism. Closing every loop immediately removes the reason to keep going.
- Retention built entirely on withheld information collapses the moment the answer arrives — it needs emotional or structural support underneath it to survive resolution.

## Key Concepts
- Continuation points, not just the opening hook
- Open-loop retention
- Retention that survives resolution
