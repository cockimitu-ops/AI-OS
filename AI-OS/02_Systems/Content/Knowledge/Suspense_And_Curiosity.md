# Suspense and Curiosity

Purpose: The mechanics of sustained tension — how attention is held between a hook and a resolution.

## Core Principles
- Suspense comes from knowing a threat exists and not knowing its outcome; curiosity comes from not knowing a fact yet. They're distinct mechanisms that combine: curiosity about what already happened, suspense about what happens next.
- Withholding information only works if the audience knows something is being withheld — silent omission isn't suspense, it's just absence.
- Escalation, not repetition, sustains tension. Restating the same stakes reads as stalling, not building.

## Key Concepts
- Suspense vs. curiosity as distinct mechanisms
- Visible withholding
- Escalation vs. repetition
