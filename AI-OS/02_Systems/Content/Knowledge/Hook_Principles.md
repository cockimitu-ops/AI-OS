## Core Principles
- A hook creates a question, not an answer — curiosity requires an open loop.
- The strongest hooks imply stakes immediately: something to lose, something at risk.
- A hook that resolves too early gives the audience permission to leave.
- Specific, concrete images outperform vague setup — "she found a letter under the floorboard" beats "something strange happened."

## Key Concepts
- Open loop / curiosity gap
- Implied stakes
- Premature resolution (a failure mode, not a style choice)
