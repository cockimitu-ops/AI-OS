## Core Principles
- Style should serve the content's purpose, not the writer's preference — a tense scene and a reflective one call for different sentence rhythm.
- Shorter sentences read as urgent; longer sentences read as reflective or explanatory. Deliberately mixing both creates rhythm.
- Concrete nouns and active verbs carry more information per word than abstract nouns and passive constructions.

## Key Concepts
- Sentence rhythm
- Concrete vs. abstract language
- Active vs. passive voice
