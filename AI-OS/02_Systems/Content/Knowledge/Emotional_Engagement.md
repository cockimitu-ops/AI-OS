## Core Principles
- Audiences invest emotionally in outcomes they've been made to want, not outcomes merely described to them.
- Specific, sensory detail produces a stronger response than abstract naming of an emotion — "her hands were shaking" outperforms "she was scared."
- Emotional variety, not just escalating intensity of one emotion, sustains engagement longer than a single sustained note.

## Key Concepts
- Earned investment vs. told investment
- Show over tell, applied specifically to emotion
- Emotional variety
