## Core Principles
- Editing is subtractive by default — cutting a weak sentence improves a piece more often than adding a stronger one.
- Read for one concern per editing pass (clarity, then pacing, then style) rather than all at once. Simultaneous editing misses more than sequential editing.
- If a sentence's purpose can't be named, it's a cut candidate regardless of how well-written it is — see the sentence-purpose rule in [[Reddit_Story_Workflow]].

## Key Concepts
- Subtractive editing
- Single-concern passes
- Purpose test for cuts
