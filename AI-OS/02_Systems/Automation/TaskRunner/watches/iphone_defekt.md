Higher price ceiling than the Android watch: a working iPhone resells for enough
that even a 100 EUR broken one can clear margin.

The lock excludes are deliberate, not noise filtering: an iCloud-locked iPhone
is unsellable and is a stolen-device signal - see the README. Never buy one, so
never get pinged about one.

Note they are *phrases*, not the bare word `icloud`. Tested live 2026-08-31, a
bare `icloud` exclude silently dropped "iPhone 13 Bastler - iCloud frei" - an ad
advertising the exact opposite, i.e. one of the good ones. Substring excludes on
a single ambiguous word cut both ways.
