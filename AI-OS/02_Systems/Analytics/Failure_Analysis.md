# Failure Analysis

Purpose: How to determine why a project didn't meet its Success Criteria.
Last Updated: 2026-08-07
Status: Active
Related Documents: [[02_Systems/Analytics/README|Analytics]], [[Experiment_Tracking]], [[Viral_Analysis]]
Required Notes: [[Metrics_Framework]], [[Success_Criteria]]

---

A failure has a cause, not always the thing that looks broken — separating which is the job. Don't stop at the first plausible reason; check whether it would predict other metrics that also didn't move. A cause that can't be nailed down is recorded as inconclusive, not force-fit — a valid outcome, see [[Experiment_Tracking]].

In: a metric that missed its threshold. Out: a named cause, or "inconclusive."
