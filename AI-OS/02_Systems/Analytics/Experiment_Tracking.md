# Experiment Tracking

Purpose: How a hypothesis gets tracked from proposal through result.
Last Updated: 2026-08-07
Status: Active
Related Documents: [[02_Systems/Analytics/README|Analytics]], [[Knowledge_Promotion]], [[Learning_Extraction]]
Required Notes: [[Success_Criteria]], [[Failure_Analysis]]

---

Governs tracking once an experiment exists — [[Knowledge_Promotion]] defines what makes something an experiment. Every tracked experiment records what it expected, what happened, and decisive-or-ambiguous; ambiguous is recorded, not deleted. A successful experiment doesn't self-promote — its result is a [[Learning_Extraction]] candidate, not an automatic knowledge update.

In: a hypothesis, its Success Criteria, its outcome. Out: expected vs. actual, decisive or ambiguous.
