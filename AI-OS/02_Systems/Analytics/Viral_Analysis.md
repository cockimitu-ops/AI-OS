# Viral Analysis

Purpose: Why a project significantly exceeded its Success Criteria — the positive-outlier counterpart to Failure Analysis.
Last Updated: 2026-08-07
Status: Active
Related Documents: [[02_Systems/Analytics/README|Analytics]], [[Failure_Analysis]], [[Experiment_Tracking]]
Required Notes: [[Metrics_Framework]], [[Success_Criteria]]

---

An outlier is just as easy to misattribute as a failure, with added risk — success invites more confidence, making a wrong attribution more likely to get promoted before it's earned. Check external factors (posting time, algorithm, a trending topic) before crediting the content. One viral result is an [[Experiment_Tracking]] candidate — try to reproduce it — not an immediate promotion; a single hit isn't "reproducible in principle" yet.

In: a metric that significantly exceeded threshold. Out: a named factor (content or external), or "insufficient evidence."
